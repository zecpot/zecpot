    How zecPOT works

    One pot a day
    Tickets cost 0.01 ZEC and you can buy as many as you like. Once a day one ticket is drawn and takes the whole pot, minus 5% for the treasury and the Zcash network fee.
    The 5% sweeps the floor
    The treasury buys the cheapest zkSNARK on Zilkroad whenever it can afford one. Every week, one of those zkSNARKs is drawn for a random ticket from that week’s pots.
    No accounts
    A ticket is a key made in your browser. Its public half rides in the payment memo; the secret half is your ticket code.
    No sender data
    Shielded payments hide who paid. The pool sees an amount and a memo, nothing else.
    Anyone can audit the money
    The pot’s and the treasury’s viewing keys are public. Load them into a view-only wallet to see every ticket that came in, every fee, and every sweep.
    Nobody picks the winner
    The ticket list locks at the closing block. The winner comes from the hash of a block mined after that, so anyone can recompute it.
    Claim without an account
    The winner signs a shielded address with their ticket code and the prize is sent there. Only a hash of the address is published. Unclaimed prizes roll into a later pot after 14 days.
