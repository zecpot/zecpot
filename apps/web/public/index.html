Two steps to get your tickets

  1. Save your ticket code

    It proves the tickets are yours, and it’s the only way to claim a win. This browser keeps a copy, but save it somewhere else too. We never see it and can’t recover it.

Get ZEC with USDC or USDT

Swap stablecoins into your own shielded Zcash wallet, then pay the pot from there. The swap goes through NEAR Intents straight from this page. Our server never sees it.

Send from an exchange or another wallet

This swap is public on the sending network, and NEAR Intents records which Zcash address it paid. Paying the pot afterwards is shielded and isn’t linked to the swap. To make the two harder to match up, swap a bit more than you need and wait a while before you pay.

How it works

  1. Buy tickets

    0.01 ZEC each, as many as you like, in one shielded payment. No account and no sign-up.

  2. One ticket is drawn each day

    When the pot closes, a block mined afterwards picks the winner. Nobody, not even us, can choose it or know it in advance.

  3. The winner claims the pot

    Paste your ticket code and a shielded address. The whole pot, minus 5% and the network fee, is sent there in ZEC.

Privacy and fairness, in detail
No sender data
Shielded payments hide who paid. The pot sees an amount and a memo, nothing else.
Tickets are keys
Your browser makes a key for each purchase. The public half goes in the payment memo. The secret half is your ticket code, and it never leaves your device.
Anyone can check the draw
The ticket list locks at the closing block. The winner comes from the hash of a block mined after that. This page re-checks every past draw itself, and anyone can run the same check with npm run verify.
Anyone can audit the money
The viewing keys for the pot and the treasury are public. Load them into a view-only wallet to see every ticket, fee and zkSNARK purchase.
Winners stay private
Only a hash of the payout address is published. Unclaimed prizes roll into a later pot after 14 days.
