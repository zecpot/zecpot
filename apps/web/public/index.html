    How it stays private and fair

    No accounts
    A ticket is a key made in your browser. Its public half rides in the payment memo; the secret half is your ticket code.
    No sender data
    Shielded payments hide who paid. The pool sees an amount and a memo, nothing else.
    Anyone can audit the pot
    The pool’s viewing key is public. Load it into a view-only wallet to see every ticket that came in.
    Nobody picks the winner
    The ticket list locks at the closing block. The winner comes from the hash of a block mined after that, so anyone can recompute it.
    No stuck money
    If a round closes before its pot covers the prize, your tickets move into the next round on their own. Until that round closes, you can take your ZEC back instead, minus the network fee.
    Delivery to a pseudonym
    The winner signs a Zilkroad account ID with their ticket code. We buy the floor zkSNARK and send it there.
